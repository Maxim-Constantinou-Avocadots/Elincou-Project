Mindray / BS-430

The system delivers highly accurate and traceable results, supported by an advanced optical grating system and tungsten halogen lamp with a 2,000-hour lifespan – ensuring exceptional reliability, accuracy, and stability. Designed for cost efficiency, it requires minimal reagent volume per test and features a dry bath for reduced maintenance. Additional capabilities include onboard auto-hemolysis for HbA1c, automatic rerun, intelligent carryover management, and enzyme linearity extension to minimize false negatives and accelerate turnaround time. Furthermore, the analyzer integrates HbA1c smart-sampling technology, enabling automatic hemolysate preparation for whole blood samples. This not only shortens turnaround time but also eliminates biohazard risks and manual handling errors.
