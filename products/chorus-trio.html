Overview

High-performance, efficient, and flexible ELISA immunoassay analyzer that requires minimal manual intervention, ensuring safe and intuitive operation. Extensive test menu spans a wide range of clinical areas.  

Supporting up to 30 tests per analytical run in monotest format, the analyzer employs the ELISA detection principle to ensure high analytical sensitivity and specificity. Its broad assay portfolio encompasses autoimmunity, infectious diseases, urinary and stool antigens, inflammatory markers, bone metabolism, complement fixation, endocrinology, tumor markers, anemia, and allergy.
