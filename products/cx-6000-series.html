Overview

Automated, efficient, and innovative coagulation analyzers equipped with advanced technology to ensure consistent, high-quality performance. These mid-range systems set new standards by delivering high throughput, an ultra-stable integrated reagent cassette, intelligent retesting, and long-lasting aspiration needles as part of the standard configuration.

These next‑generation systems combine a compact footprint with advanced automation, including automatic sample loading, continuous sample addition, and intelligent re‑examination to streamline daily workflows. They provide more than two weeks of on‑board reagent stability, use integrated reagent kits with volatility‑proof cross‑seals, and maintain independent refrigeration of the reagent tray at 2–8 °C even after shutdown.

The platforms operate with convenient tear‑and‑use liquid reagents available in 100‑ or 200‑test kit formats. Further enhancements include a Sample Quality Validation (SQV) system with HIL nine‑square assessment and an External Quality Control (EQC) system that automatically uploads daily QC data, performs statistical evaluation based on EQC rules, and delivers real‑time performance feedback.
