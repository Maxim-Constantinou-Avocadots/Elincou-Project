Overview

Fully integrated solution that manages every step of the workflow – from the pre-analytical phase through to post-analytical processes. Proprietary SF Cube® technology featured on all analyzers included in the setup. Compatible with LabXpert® software.

All-in-one solution for comprehensive cellular analysis, integrating advanced sample processing with hematology analysis featuring the full capabilities of the BC-7600 Series. It includes a fully automated slide maker and stainer, digital cell morphology powered by multi-layer fusion technology for superior imaging, and HbA1c testing using the gold-standard high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) method.

Its modular design allows configuration to meet diverse workflow requirements, with the number of analyzer units tailored to specific needs. Additionally, flexible setup options enable easy adaptation to various room layouts, including space-constrained environments. This system drives precision, automation, and efficiency in a single platform.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
