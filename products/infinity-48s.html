Cepheid / Infinity 48S

The GeneXpert Infinity system, powered by advanced 10‑color technology, enables laboratories to run an exceptionally broad test menu on a single, scalable platform. With flexible configurations that support both 6‑ and 10‑color modules, the system’s enhanced multiplexing capability allows independent monitoring of more targets, delivering actionable results that support timely clinical decisions—often in under 60 minutes. The closed‑cartridge design minimizes contamination risk while supporting true random‑access, mix‑and‑match testing for maximum operational flexibility. The GeneXpert platform, together with its continually expanding portfolio of rapid, accurate, and user‑friendly PCR assays—including advanced multiplex and syndromic panels—provides clinicians with clinically actionable, on‑demand results across a wide spectrum of disease areas, all within a unified and scalable system.
