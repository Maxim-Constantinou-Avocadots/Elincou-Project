Overview

Innovative, compact, cost-efficient analyzer along with user-friendly software for CBC + 5 part results. An accurate and efficient solution for labs.

Requires only 3 reagents, 2 of which are stored inside the analyzer, thus making it more compact and cost-efficient. Also, the BC-5150 supports bi-directional LIS with test results and patient information. Furthermore, it inherits its convenient and proven, powerful software design with an intuitive interface from the BC-6800 platform.

Technical specifications
