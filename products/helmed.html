AESKU / HELMED

HELMED brings together the three major laboratory techniques- IFA, ELISA, and BLOT- within a compact 1m3 footprint. Its three-needle and three independent processing rings reduce overall assay time and enhance result reproducibility. A built-in barcode reader enables automatic detection and scanning of sample tube IDs, eliminating transcription errors and improving workflow reliability. Before each run, HELMED’s green protective cover locks automatically, creating a controlled and reproducible internal environment that shields assay from external interference. Despite its small size, the system’s IFA module can process up to 190 samples in a single run and supports both screening and titration from a single worklist. IFA screening can be confirmed directly on the same instrument through the BLOT and ELISA modules. None of the techniques require sample pre-dilution, further simplifying operation and reducing hands-on time.
