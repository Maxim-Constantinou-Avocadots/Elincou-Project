Mindray / BC-7600 Series

Performs PLT-H and PLT-O in every CD mode to help remove the interference of fragmented RBC/microcyte RBC/large PLT during PLT counting. What distinguishes the BC-7600 Series is its advanced diagnostic capabilities; beyond the traditional Mindray disease-related flags that assist clinical screening, this series offers additional flagging for the detection of Infectious Mononucleosis (IM). Moreover, during general CBC counting, the system conducts Hematological Malignant Cell screening, enabling early identification of abnormal promyelocytes that may appear in the peripheral blood of patients with acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL). These analyzers are also adapted for use in Blood Banks, offering dedicated Blood Bank modes.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
