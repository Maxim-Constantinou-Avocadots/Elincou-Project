Mindray / BS-600M

A compact system with a footprint of just 1.2m2 provides flexible workflow management with continuous rack loading and STAT priority for urgent samples. For HbA1c testing, onboard whole‑blood hemolysis streamlines processing by eliminating manual pretreatment, reducing biohazard exposure, and minimizing human error. With a throughput of 120 HbA1c tests per hour, turnaround times are significantly reduced, and all results remain fully traceable to IFCC/NGSP standards. Accuracy is further strengthened by the PDR Optical Platform®, which delivers panoramic, real‑time monitoring and signal traceback to eliminate interference and noise throughout the reaction process. A user‑friendly design requiring no daily maintenance, combined with an intelligent interface and breathing LED indicators, enhances operational ease. High‑level Sigma metric performance ensures superior result quality at lower cost, boosting clinical confidence and satisfaction. Proprietary FS‑Sampling® (Full Scene Sampling) technology identifies sample containers using advanced databases and algorithms to ensure highly accurate aspiration.
