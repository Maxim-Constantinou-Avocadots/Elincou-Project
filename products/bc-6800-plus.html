Overview

Highest-throughput hematology analyzer out there. Reliable routine CBC + 5-part DIFF results. Proprietary SF Cube® technology for more refined and reliable cell differentiation. As the flagship of hematology analyzers, it sets the benchmark for performance and innovation. Compatible with LabXpert® software.

The BC-6800 Plus delivers greater clinical value with higher flagging efficiency, helping reduce the need for microscopic examination. It supports CBC + ESR joint testing and provides NRBC, RET, and Body Fluid results in a high-speed system – all while maintaining cost-effectiveness. Designed to meet all the demands of modern laboratories, the BC-6800 Plus not only fulfills requirements but also exceeds expectations in terms of value, operational efficiency, and affordability.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
