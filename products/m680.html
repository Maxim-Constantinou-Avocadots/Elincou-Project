Overview

Reliable, robust, and fully integrated chemistry and immunoassay solution, engineered to boost workflow efficiency while minimizing maintenance needs and reducing service costs.

Occupying just 2.2 m2, the system integrates chemistry, immunoassay, ISE, and whole-blood HbA1c testing in one compact platform. It offers flexible management with continuous rack loading and STAT priority for urgent samples. For HbA1c, onboard hemolysis of whole blood enhances workflow

efficiency, eliminating manual pretreatment to reduce biohazard risks and human error. With a throughput of 120 HbA1c tests per hour, turnaround time is significantly shortened, and all results are traceable to IFCC/NGSP standards. Accuracy is reinforced by the PDR Optical Platform® , delivering panoramic, real-time monitoring and signal traceback to eliminate interference and noise during reactions for consistently precise results.

The user-friendly design requires no daily maintenance and features an intelligent interface and breathing LED indicators. High-level Sigma metric performance ensures laboratories deliver superior quality results at reduced cost, enhancing clinical confidence and satisfaction. Proprietary FS-Sampling® (Full Scene Sampling) technology identifies sample containers using advanced databases and algorithms, improving aspiration accuracy.

Finally, the probe is inserted into a dedicated chamber, receiving 360° waterfall vacuum washing and enhanced cleaning to reduce carryover to less than 0.1 ppm.
