Overview

Fully automated ESR system based on the modified Westergren method, designed to perform testing directly in EDTA tubes. It supports continuous, random-access loading and requires only minimal sample volume, ensuring streamlined and efficient operation. 

With a throughput of 190 tests per hour and capacity for 18 racks of 10 samples, the system is built for demanding laboratory environments. The 89-position analysis module maximizes workflow efficiency, while a dedicated method for 0.5 ml pediatric/low-volume samples allows direct measurement with no sample manipulation. It supports continuous, random-access loading of virtually any cell-counter rack, ensuring uninterrupted operation.

Upcoming Artificial Intelligence-based recognition of lipemic, hemolyzed, coagulated, and mislabeled samples.
