Mindray / CL-2600i

Proprietary FS-Sampling® (Full Scene Sampling) technology leverages databases and advanced algorithms to identify anomalies detected by the probe – such as reagent bubbles or clots – ensuring accurate sample collection without needle extraction and maintaining smooth, uninterrupted operation. Additionally, the probe is inserted in a special chamber that delivers a 360° waterfall vacuum wash combined with an enhanced Alkaline wash, effectively eliminating carry-over to less than 0.1 ppm. Moreover, the system applies ultrasonic energy to break fibrin in the sample. Continuous loading support allows for true walk-away operation.
