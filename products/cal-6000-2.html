DIESSE / Chorus EVO

Designed for high flexibility and efficiency, the system provides 48 sample positions and the capacity for 86 individual monotest reagents, ensuring maximum productivity. Its random-access capability streamlines laboratory workflow and reduces turnaround time.

With ELISA, CLIA, and Macroarray technologies integrated into one platform, the analyzer easily performs multiple test methods simultaneously and supports comprehensive diagnostic profiles in a single run.

Its extensive test menu covers a wide array of clinical specialties, including autoimmunity, infectious diseases, inflammatory markers, stool antigens, urinary antigens, biological drug monitoring, tumor markers, anemia, allergy, bone metabolism, and neurodegenerative diseases.
