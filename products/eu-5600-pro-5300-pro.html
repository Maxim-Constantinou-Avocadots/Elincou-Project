Overview

Precise, advanced, and automated urinalysis system designed to maximize efficiency while virtually eliminating the need for manual microscopic review. Compatible with LabXpert® software.

The system combines dry chemistry, automated formed element analysis, and RBC phase analysis to significantly enhance time and space management efficiency. Equipped with 2K full-color imaging that closely replicates manual microscopy, it enables report generation without the need for manual microscopic review of positive samples.

Additionally, the system captures up to 60 high-resolution images from the sample and employs advanced algorithms to accurately identify various formed elements from panoramic views, reducing the risk of missed positive findings. It automatically provides detailed parameters for different urinary RBC types, including histograms that clearly illustrate cell size, shape, and hemoglobin content.

By analyzing RBC morphological variations and leveraging high-quality images, the system supports rapid and precise identification of hematuria sources through a comprehensive review and validation process.

Furthermore, it accommodates closed-tube urine sample processing, ensuring superior biosafety standards.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
