Overview

The GeneXpert System delivers fully automated PCR testing across flexible 2‑, 4‑, and 16‑module configurations. Each platform is built around the same proven GeneXpert analytical module and uses the patented, single‑use Xpert® cartridge technology to ensure consistent, reliable performance across the entire test menu.

GeneXpert systems are advanced molecular analyzers designed to deliver rapid, reliable results—often in about an hour, including minimum sample preparation. The patented Xpert® cartridge technology supports an extensive test menu and accommodates a wide range of sample types, while the compact, consolidated system design saves valuable laboratory space and reduces the need for multiple platforms.

These next‑generation systems are also stackable, offering flexible scalability. Each GeneXpert platform fully integrates and automates sample extraction, amplification, and detection within a single cartridge. Modular configurations allow a two‑site system to operate with 1 or 2 modules, a four‑site system with 1 to 4 modules, and a sixteen‑site system with 4, 8, 12, or 16 modules, ensuring adaptable performance for laboratories of any size.
