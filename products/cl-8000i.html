Overview

Massive throughput, intelligent, fully automated chemiluminescence immunoassay analyzer that maximizes efficiency and productivity. Offers various setup options, including the option to be integrated with a biochemistry analyzer. Requires no daily maintenance.

Achieve exceptional efficiency with an impressive throughput of up to 500 tests/hour. The system incorporates built-in intelligence to help predict reagents and consumables, helping optimize workflows and minimize interruptions – all with the user experience in mind. Proprietary FS-Sampling® (Full Scene Sampling) technology harnesses advanced algorithms and comprehensive databases to detect anomalies such as reagent bubbles and clots.

This ensures precise sample collection without needle extraction, maintaining smooth and uninterrupted operation. Additionally, the probe is inserted in a special chamber that delivers a 360° waterfall vacuum wash

combined with an enhanced Alkaline wash, effectively eliminating carry-over. For maximum convenience, the system supports continuous loading for true walk-away operation, while a dedicated priority channel ensures urgent samples are processed without delay. Patented lock-screen design allows real-time monitoring of analyzer operation.
