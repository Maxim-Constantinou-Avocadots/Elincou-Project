Overview

Accurate, Automatic analyzer with integrated CBC + ESR detection. Proprietary SF Cube® technology for more refined and reliable cell differentiation. Compatible with LabXpert® software.

Performs, without any additional cost, NRBC in every test to ensure the reliability and accuracy of WBC result and help diagnosis for anemia and optical PLT-H in every CD mode to help remove the interference of fragmented RBC/microcyte RBC/large PLT during PLT counting. Also performs CBC + ESR combined examination, using only one tube of blood sample, to provide an efficient and convenient tool for inflammation diagnosis.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
