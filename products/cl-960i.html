Overview

Compact, fully automated chemiluminescence immunoassay analyzer that combines a large capacity and fast assay speed into a small form factor, while ensuring minimal maintenance requirements.

Performs routine immunoassay tests with high sensitivity using enhanced ALP-AMPPD chemiluminescence technology and magnetic particle separation, without requiring additional infrastructure or complex setup. The analyzer delivers up to 180 tests per hour and supports continuous loading for true walk-away operation.

Beyond its broad test menu – including thyroid, fertility, tumor markers, cardiac, diabetes, and infectious disease panels – it ensures long calibration stability of 28 days and cost-effective operation with ready-to-use reagents. What sets the CL-960i apart is its compact design combined with premium performance, making it an ideal solution for labs seeking speed, accuracy, and efficiency without sacrificing space or budget.
