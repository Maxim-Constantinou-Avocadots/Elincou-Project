AESKU / HELIOS

HELIOS automated IFA system combines an all-in-one IFA processor and reader with the distinction of being the only platform on the market that ensures IFA processing meets ISO, FDA, and WHO standards. It supports a broad menu of autoimmune and infectious-disease serology tests, requires no pre-dilution, and initiates reading automatically. Before each run, HELIOS automatically locks its protective cover, maintaining a controlled, reproducible environment that safeguards the assay from external interference. A comprehensive onboard pattern library, including the ICAP library, enhances interpretation accuracy, while well-by-well liquid management maintains consistent incubation times and prevents cross-contamination.
