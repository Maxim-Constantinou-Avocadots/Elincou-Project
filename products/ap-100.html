Overview

Compact, Automated Dosing System, designed for accurate and safe dosing of Mindray ID/AST.

Multi-location technology helps to reduce manual intervention and improve both accuracy and speed. The compact design allows for plate dosing in the biosafety cabinet, which ensures higher safety during operation.
