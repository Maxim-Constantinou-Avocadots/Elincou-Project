Overview

Automatic, continuous-loading ESR system capable of performing four tests at the same time directly from the EDTA tube.  With a micro-volume requirement of only 0.5 ml, it provides rapid, reliable results in only 20 minutes. 

Equipped with four clearly marked cuvette positions and continuous sample loading, the system ensures rapid turnaround, providing complete ESR results in 20 minutes. An optional correction function automatically adjusts ESR values for samples with hematocrit levels under 40%. The test is carried out directly on routine lavender‑top EDTA tubes, eliminating the need for special collection tubes.
