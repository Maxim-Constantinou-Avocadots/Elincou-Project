Overview

Compact, efficient, versatile benchtop chemistry analyzer, featuring advanced features and capabilities that set it apart from comparable products.

The analyzer incorporates cutting-edge technology for superior performance. Its smart-sampling technology automates hemolysate preparation for HbA1c testing, while pretreatment-free operation maximizes productivity. A built-in step-by-step maintenance guide ensures effortless use, and the advanced auto-washing system minimizes carryover and reduces water consumption. Despite its compact design, the analyzer delivers impressive throughput. Additionally, the BS-240Pro is equipped with an intelligent probe offering optional clog detection for enhanced reliability.
