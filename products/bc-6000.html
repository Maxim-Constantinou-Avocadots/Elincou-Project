Mindray / BC-6000

Measures in routine CBC, the actual number of nucleated red blood cells (NRBCs), if they are present in the sample, without any additional reagents or extra costs. The analyzer also automatically calculates the neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR) parameter in each CBC + DIFF. Apart from the 5-part, it also introduces InR (information about malaria) as a parameter to test for malaria, hence the 6-part terminology that is often used.

LabXpert® Software: streamlines laboratory workflows and optimizes data analysis with advanced features such as improved re-examination efficiency and auto-validation for normal samples. It also offers an intuitive interface for the review and validation of pathological samples, making complex processes simpler and more efficient.
