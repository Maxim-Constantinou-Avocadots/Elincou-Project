Mindray / BS-1000M

BS-1000M is a versatile chemistry analyzer capable of whole-blood HbA1c testing, photometric chemistry assays, and operation with an integrated ISE module. It supports an extensive menu of clinical chemistry assays and enhances biosafety with an integrated auto-decapper. The system can also be integrated with the CL-2600i to form M980 immunoassay modules to create a unified, high-efficiency workflow.
